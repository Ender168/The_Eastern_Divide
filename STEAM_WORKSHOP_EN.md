[h1]The Eastern Divide[/h1]
[b]A standalone two-player scenario for Hearts of Iron IV, set across Eastern Europe.[/b]

Choose the Western or Eastern Side and fight across a narrow opening front that can expand as the campaign develops. Each side begins with 25 key cities. Capture and hold your opponent's cities to meet the conditions for victory.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Two playable sides, WEF and EEF, with neutral territories between and around them.
[*]25 key cities for each side, marked on the map with 10 victory points.
[*]Standard and fast victory conditions, with requirements that decrease over time.
[*]Timed missions that track control of key cities.
[*]Decisions to deploy fully equipped test armies when a side is human-controlled.
[*]A rebuilt railway and supply-hub network with reserve routes for both sides.
[*]English and Russian localization.
[/list]

This is an original, independently developed scenario using the vanilla Hearts of Iron IV map.

[b]Game version:[/b] The current descriptor targets Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.*. Check compatibility with your installed game version before starting a multiplayer match.

The supply network has been checked statically. In-game behavior and multiplayer balance are still being tested.

[h1]Update: Supply Network[/h1]
This update introduces a more resilient railway network for both sides.

[list]
[*]Added six level-four railway links for WEF and five for EEF.
[*]Added reserve routes through the center, allowing WEF to retain more of its supply network after losing key hubs.
[*]Improved worst-case network resilience for both sides under two railway breaks.
[*]Preserved the existing supply hubs and scenario setup.
[*]Added clearer English and Russian victory-timer descriptions and hid internal script flags from decision tooltips.
[/list]

These changes improve the network's theoretical resilience to railway cuts. Actual supply flow and multiplayer balance still need in-game testing.
