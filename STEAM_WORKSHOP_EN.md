## Mod description

**The Eastern Divide** is a standalone two-player scenario for Hearts of Iron IV, set across Eastern Europe.

Choose the Western or Eastern Side and fight across a narrow opening front that can expand as the campaign develops. Each side begins with 25 key cities. Capture and hold your opponent's cities to meet the conditions for victory.

The scenario features:

- Two playable sides, WEF and EEF, with neutral territories between and around them.
- Standard and fast victory conditions, with requirements that decrease over time.
- Timed missions that track control of key cities.
- Decisions for deploying fully equipped test armies when a side is human-controlled.
- A rebuilt railway and supply-hub network with reserve routes for both sides.
- English and Russian localization.

This is an original, independently developed scenario. It uses the vanilla Hearts of Iron IV map.

**Game version:** The current descriptor targets Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.*. Please check compatibility with your installed version before starting a multiplayer game.

The supply network has been checked statically. In-game behavior and multiplayer balance are still being tested.

## Update description

**The Eastern Divide — Supply Network Update**

This update introduces a rebuilt supply network for both sides.

- Added six level-four railway links for WEF and five for EEF.
- Added reserve routes through the center so WEF can retain more of its supply network after losing key hubs.
- Improved the worst-case network resilience under two railway breaks for both sides.
- Kept the existing supply hubs and scenario setup intact.
- Added clearer English and Russian descriptions for victory timers and hid internal script flags from decision tooltips.

The changes improve the network's theoretical ability to withstand rail cuts. Actual supply flow and multiplayer balance still need to be tested in game.
