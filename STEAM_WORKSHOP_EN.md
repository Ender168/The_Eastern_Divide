[h1]The Eastern Divide[/h1]
[b]A standalone two-player scenario for Hearts of Iron IV, set across Eastern Europe.[/b]

Choose the Western or Eastern Side and fight across a narrow opening front that can expand as the campaign develops. Each side begins with 25 key cities. Capture and hold your opponent's cities to meet the conditions for victory.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Two playable sides, WEF and EEF, with neutral territories between and around them.
[*]25 key cities for each side, marked on the map with 10 victory points.
[*]Standard and fast victory conditions, with requirements that decrease over time.
[*]Timed missions that track control of key cities.
[*]Decisions to deploy fully equipped test armies when a side is human-controlled.
[*]A rebuilt railway and supply-hub network with reserve routes for both sides.
[*]English and Russian localization.
[/list]

This is an original, independently developed scenario using the vanilla Hearts of Iron IV map.

[b]Game version:[/b] The current descriptor targets Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.*. Check compatibility with your installed game version before starting a multiplayer match.

The supply network has been checked statically. In-game behavior and multiplayer balance are still being tested.

[h1]Update notes[/h1]
This is the first release. The following scenario content and systems have been introduced:

[list]
[*]Created a standalone Eastern European scenario for two opposing player sides, WEF and EEF, with neutral territories.
[*]Reworked the playable map setup, territorial assignments, and opening front.
[*]Added 25 key cities for each side. Key cities are marked with 10 victory points; other settlements have 1.
[*]Added English and Russian scenario, decision, and victory-condition localization.
[*]Removed the vanilla decisions from the scenario.
[*]Removed starting armies and added human-only decisions to deploy each side's fully equipped test force of 300 infantry and 30 tank divisions.
[*]Added the standard victory condition. It starts at 25 enemy key cities, drops by one every 105 days to a minimum of one, and requires holding the threshold for 150 days.
[*]Added the fast victory condition. It starts at 25 enemy key cities, drops by one every 225 days to a minimum of one, and requires holding the threshold for 60 days.
[*]Added hidden monthly control checks and timed missions for victory progress.
[*]Added automatic match resolution by event when a victory condition is completed.
[*]Added post-match handling to disband the defeated side's divisions, send its aircraft to storage, and demilitarize its territory.
[*]Rebuilt the railway and supply-hub network and added six level-four reserve railway links for WEF and five for EEF.
[*]Added a decision category for victory conditions with live key-city control counts and descriptions of each victory mode.
[/list]
